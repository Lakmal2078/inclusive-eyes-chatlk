# User Manual

Fast Cash lets 1xBet players in Sri Lanka request deposits and withdrawals through an agent. 18+ only.

## Language and theme

Use the language switcher in the header for **English / සිංහල / தமிழ்**, and the theme toggle for dark or light mode. Both choices are remembered on your device.

## Finding your Player ID

Open the 1xBet app → your account/profile section. The numeric ID there is your Player ID. Every request needs it.

## Creating an account (optional but recommended)

1. Go to **Register**.
2. Enter your name, email, password and Player ID.
3. Open the confirmation email and click the link.
4. Sign in at **Login**.

With an account you can see your request history and statuses. Without one you can still submit a deposit or withdrawal, but you won't be able to track it in the app.

## Making a deposit

1. Open **Deposit**.
2. Pick an agent account from the list and copy the account number (copy button next to it).
3. Transfer your amount to that account from your bank or wallet app.
4. Back in Fast Cash, fill in: amount, Player ID, payment method, receipt reference.
5. Optionally upload the receipt image — the scanner reads the amount and reference automatically; check the values it filled in and correct anything wrong.
6. Submit. You get a reference number like `TXN1A2B3C4D` — keep it.
7. Optionally send the reference on WhatsApp to speed up the review.

Amount must be within the displayed minimum and maximum.

## Making a withdrawal

1. In the 1xBet app choose **Cash** withdrawal (City: Walasmulla, Street: Beliatta Road 24/7) and get the security code.
2. Open **Withdraw** in Fast Cash.
3. Enter amount, Player ID, security code, your full name, bank, account number and contact number.
4. Submit and keep the reference number.

Check your account number twice — payouts go exactly where you type.

## Tracking a request

Open **Transactions** (signed in) to see each request with its status:

| Status    | Meaning                                      |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| PENDING   | Waiting for agent review                     |
| APPROVED  | Agent verified it, payout/credit in progress |
| COMPLETED | Money credited or paid out                   |
| REJECTED  | Could not be processed — contact support     |

## Support

- **Support** page: chat for common questions about deposits, withdrawals, Player ID and timing
- **WhatsApp** button: reach an agent directly during service hours; include your reference number

Requests are reviewed during service hours, not instantly.

## Accessibility

- Every control is reachable by keyboard, with a visible focus ring
- Menus and dialogs announce their state; Escape closes dialogs
- Statuses are labelled in text, not only colour
- Page language is announced correctly in all three languages

## Safety

- Only transfer to accounts listed on the Deposit page in the app. They can change — check every time.
- Fast Cash staff never ask for your 1xBet password.
- Gambling is for entertainment only. Set limits, never bet borrowed money, and stop if it stops being fun. See the Privacy Policy and responsible-gambling notice.
